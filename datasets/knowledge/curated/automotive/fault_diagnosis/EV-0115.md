# IONIQ5 9B5充电中断逻辑适用于特定AC场景

## 知识概述

24-01-051H-1描述特定IONIQ5在240V AC Level2充电中断或降速以保护连接，VCMS改进逻辑可在稳定后自恢复充电能力。

## 适用范围与限制

厂商：Hyundai；型号/系列：IONIQ 5；版本：24-01-051H-1；地区：North America。

仅解释该来源明确列出的车型、手册版本和市场。托管网站国家不替代车型市场适用性，需核本车VIN、年款、配置与软件。 技术公告有特定生产日期、ECU/ROM和活动条件，打印或缓存副本不保证为当前最新服务版本。 当前范围仅IONIQ 5 NE1，生产2023-10-04至2023-12-18的所列车辆；以VIN开放活动和当前厂商系统再核验。

> CE1) and 2024-
> 2025MY Kona Electric (SX2 EV) vehicles may exhibit a condition while charging with a 240V AC
> (Level 2) charger where the vehicle experiences an interrupted charging session or a lower charging
> speed. This occurs to prevent potential damage to the charging cable connection. Follow the
> procedure in this TSB to update the software logic for the Vehicle Charging Management System
> (VCMS), which initiates a self-restart that allows the vehicle charging to return to full capacity once
> stabilized.
> Applicable Vehicles (Certain):
> • 2022-2024MY IONIQ 5 (NE1) produced 10/4/2023 – 12/18/2023
> • 2023-2024MY IONIQ 6 (CE1) produced 12/14/2022 – 12/25/2023
> • 2024-2025MY Kona Electric (SX2 EV) produced
